Zelefin

Privacy

Zelefin is a native Jellyfin client. It talks to the Jellyfin server you type in. There is no Zelefin account.

What stays on your devices

Sign-in details, library cache, downloads, and settings live on your iPhone or iPad. Playback goes to your Jellyfin. We do not operate that server.

Notifications

If you install the Zelefin plugin on Jellyfin and allow alerts, the app registers an Apple Push token with that plugin. The plugin sends alerts through Zelefin’s push service so they can arrive while the app is closed. That service holds Apple Push credentials for the Zelefin app. It receives device tokens and notification text from your plugin. It does not receive your Jellyfin password or library files.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information.
  • We do not use your library for advertising.
  • We do not require a cloud login to use Zelefin with your own server.
